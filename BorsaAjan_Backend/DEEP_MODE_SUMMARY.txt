📊 NEW TECHNICAL INDICATORS
================================================================================

SMA200:
  - Purpose: Long-term trend (institutional standard)
  - Bullish: Price > SMA200
  - Bearish: Price < SMA200

MACD (12, 26, 9):
  - Purpose: Momentum and divergence
  - Bullish: MACD > Signal (positive histogram)
  - Bearish: MACD < Signal (negative histogram)
  - Divergence: Price up but MACD down → reversal signal

Bollinger Bands (20-day, ±2σ):
  - Purpose: Volatility and mean reversion
  - Upper Band: Price > BB Upper → overbought
  - Lower Band: Price < BB Lower → oversold
